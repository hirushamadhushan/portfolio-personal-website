About Me

I like problems that reach real people.

I'm Hirusha Madhushan, a Full Stack Developer and HNDIT student at SLIATE Anuradhapura. I care less about collecting frameworks and more about whether the thing I built actually works for the person using it.

That's why MediSync exists — a smart OPD queue and centralized patient history system modelled on how Sri Lankan government hospitals really run, with live queue displays, cross-clinic medical history and a full Sinhala translation. I designed, built and deployed it to production on my own — it's running live at medisynclk.dev.

I also enjoy building things the hard way when it teaches me something. Madhushan Tech Store runs on an MVC framework I wrote myself — no Laravel, no Composer, no dependencies — with CSRF on every POST, prepared statements everywhere and hardened uploads, containerised with Docker and shipped to Railway. And on the data side, my heart disease predictor compares five algorithms and ships the winner behind a Flask app rather than stopping at the notebook.
